The answer you didn't want

The trade-in credit is the wrong thing to shop on.

Verizon advertises up to $1,200 off. That credit is paid back to you $33 at a time over 36 months, and the plan tier that unlocks the biggest number costs $25/month more than the cheapest one. Over the same 36 months that surcharge is $900 — which eats most of the extra credit. Shop the monthly plan first, the credit second.

What it costs to walk away

Promo credits arrive monthly for 36 months and stop the day you leave, downgrade the plan, or pay the phone off early. Leave mid-contract and you settle the device balance and forfeit every credit you hadn't received yet. Only the option with no device contract lets you walk.

Data, and how much to trust it

  • CERTAINiPhone 18 Pro starts at $1,199 and 18 Pro Max at $1,299, 256 GB, announced 9 Sept 2026, on sale 18 Sept. The standard iPhone 18 was not in the fall line-up — it arrives spring 2027 with the iPhone Air 2 and 18e.
  • CERTAINVerizon's headline offers: up to $1,200 off when you add a line on an eligible myPlan, up to $1,020 off when you upgrade an existing line. Paid as equal bill credits across a 36-month 0% device payment plan. Trade-in must be a tier-one Apple, Samsung, Google or Motorola device, active on the account 60+ days.
  • CERTAINSimplicity Pro bundles the iPhone 18 Pro and unlimited service at $80/mo ($30 plan + $50 device) after Auto Pay and switcher credits, plus tax. Unlimited 5G Ultra Wideband, 10 GB hotspot, Canada/Mexico roaming. Swap for the newest iPhone after 12 months or once 33% is paid off — the handset goes back in good working order.
  • CERTAINThe line must stay on myPlan for the full 36 months. Change plan and the remaining promotional value is gone.
  • LIKELYmyPlan with Auto Pay: Unlimited Welcome $65 / Plus $80 / Ultimate $90 for one line; $30 / $45 / $55 per line at four lines. Two- and three-line rates are interpolated between those published endpoints — confirm yours in the cart.
  • GUESSINGThe Welcome-tier credit ($830 default here) and the higher-storage prices. All three tiers are trade-in eligible, but Verizon reserves the top credit for Ultimate — the exact Welcome figure moves week to week. Override it in the controls with the number your cart shows.
  • LIKELYVisible is Verizon-owned and runs on Verizon's network at $25/mo with taxes and fees included. It is a different product: no store support, no perks, and base-tier data is deprioritised when a tower is busy.

Sources: Verizon newsroom · PhoneArena · TechRadar · Apple Newsroom · myPlan. Carrier promotions change weekly and vary by ZIP code and account history — treat this as a model to check a real cart against, not a quote.
